# Latency diagnostics

`python3 tests/fs2/latency.py --image sha256:IMMUTABLE_IMAGE_ID --output NEW_ABSOLUTE_PATH --phase wakeups` runs one owned synthetic diagnostic. Use a release image built from the frozen checkout with `tests/fs2/Dockerfile.linux`, `VERIFY=latency`, `BUILD_PROFILE=release`, `BIN_DIR=release`, and matching host release default/fault executables. The driver independently compares every runtime/test source with the image and records source/binary/image identities.

The wakeups phase measures 12 mounted saves each on small and warm 64 MiB backgrounds, rotating 24 B, 1 KiB and 16 KiB. Both engines use a fresh owned named disk volume for replica state; ordinary writes/read sampling execute as UID/GID 1001. There are three native saves per background and an atomic editor-save smoke. Linux processes share the VM CLOCK_MONOTONIC clock; native host/VM differences are reported as first-read upper bounds, never subtracted as cross-clock stages. This is a diagnostic in one mounted container, not the two-container final acceptance cohort or host/VM power-loss qualification.

`MAGUS_LATENCY_TRACE=1` opts into bounded-line diagnostics for durable admission, private/shared local receipts, service postcommit, authenticated discovery and successful exact Applied acknowledgement. Events carry operation/revision/head IDs and monotonic nanoseconds, without content, paths or credentials. Sender local and peer projected revision IDs may differ; the driver binds the exact peer projection to the Shared receipt head. It requires the exact service integration operation/head stamp and exact peer Applied revision/head before reporting end-to-end completion; completion is the latest of Shared commit, Applied and fresh ordinary read. Sender receipt recording may occur after peer discovery. Publisher execution-gate wait is logged separately. Public status still performs integrity checks; diagnostics do not change acknowledgement semantics.

Separate untimed boundary checks cover dropped authenticated hints with durable fallback, transport reconnect, current credential revocation/socket close, isolated private-only publication and readonly mutation denial. Raw timed attempts, transient errors, stages, receipt/base bindings, fixture hashes, source/image proofs and owned normal-unmount/resource cleanup remain in the private output. Failed attempts remain failures. Keep existing cache and prior evidence; never relabel an old image with new source.

Process-local publisher and incoming advisory epochs are shared per replica and carry no data. Durable successful admission signals publisher; authenticated WS cursors signal incoming. A 1-second fallback retains replay and authority refresh. Service subscribers register before reading their authorized durable page, then reauthorize on hints/fallback. Seed, integration (including validated integration), resolution and restore signal after committed Shared publication. No hint substitutes for durable data or current authorization. Multi-service-process notification delivery remains a later scope.

Mounted generation coalescing is 25 ms with 50 ms metadata observation; native integrity scans remain at 2 seconds. Native capture/object reuse and transport pooling belong to later latency tasks.

For an affected rerun, `--skip-boundaries` and `--skip-native-small` omit explicitly unchanged scenarios. The output labels these as not-run; prior evidence and its source bridge must be retained separately. These flags do not qualify omitted cases on new source.
